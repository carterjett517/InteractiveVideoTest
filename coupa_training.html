Module Three

Three ways to request a purchase

Coupa supports several methods to create a requisition. Each tab below covers one method — when to use it, and the exact steps to follow.

Shop a supplier site, from inside Coupa.

Punchouts are links on the homepage that open a supplier's website — think Amazon Business, Staples, or Dell. After checking out, the items automatically appear in your Coupa cart for approval.

Best for: known suppliers with large catalogs and consumer-style shopping experiences.

  • All Amazon purchases must go through the Coupa punchout — not personal or company credit cards.
  • Items are routed for approval automatically once added to cart.
  • After approval, invoices are also generated automatically.
1
Open the punchout
From your Coupa homepage, click the supplier punchout tile you need (Amazon, Staples, etc.).
2
Shop and check out
Browse the supplier site as you normally would. Add items to cart and complete checkout on their site.
3
Return to Coupa
Items appear automatically in your Coupa cart with all supplier details pre-filled.
4
Review and submit
Verify commodity and billing string, then click Submit for Approval.

Find an item with the unified search bar.

The search bar at the top of your homepage returns results across items, suppliers, previous orders, and requisitions. Start typing and Coupa surfaces matching catalog items you can add to your cart.

Best for: when you know roughly what you need but aren't tied to a specific supplier.

  • Searches return results from all approved punchout catalogs.
  • Faster than navigating to individual punchout tiles.
  • Use the same review cart workflow as punchout purchases.
1
Type in the search bar
Start typing an item name (e.g., "laptop stand") in the homepage search bar.
2
Pick a result
Click Add to Cart next to the matching catalog item you want.
3
Open the cart
Click the cart icon in the header to review your selections.
4
Submit
Update billing details if needed and submit for approval.

Request anything not in the catalog.

When the item or service you need isn't in any catalog, create a free-form request. You'll enter the supplier, description, quantity, and price manually.

Best for: services, custom orders, or one-off purchases from new or non-catalog suppliers.

  • Click the pen icon at the top of your homepage to start.
  • Complete required fields — required ones show a red asterisk or red box.
  • Approval workflow runs the same as catalog requests once submitted.
1
Click the pen icon
At the top of your homepage, click the Request Goods or Services pen icon.
2
Skip to write a request
Click Skip to open the free-form form.
3
Complete the details
Fill in item name, supplier, quantity, price, commodity, and billing string.
4
Add to cart and submit
Click Add to Cart, then Review Cart and Submit for Approval.
i
A note on watchers and approvers
At the bottom of the Review Cart screen, you can add additional Approvers (required to approve) or Watchers (have visibility, can comment, but don't block the flow). If the approval workflow shows "Pending Buyer Action," your requisition is missing a required field — fix it before submitting.

Module Four

Three ways to approve, one consistent flow

Whether you're at your desk or on the move, Coupa gives you flexible ways to act on approvals. Pick the channel that fits your workflow.

CHANNEL 01

From your To Do's

On the homepage, the To Do's section lists everything assigned to you. Click any item to see full details — then approve or reject directly. Adding a comment is required when rejecting.

CHANNEL 02

From your Inbox

Hover your name → click Inbox to see all notifications. Filter to "To-Do" to see only items requiring your action. Great for catching up after time away.

CHANNEL 03

From your Email

Every approval triggers an email with key details and three buttons: View Req, Approve, Reject. Click one — Coupa creates an auto-reply you send to finalize the action.

Watch · Approvals Deep Dive

Approvals & management hierarchy in action

Watch this demo to see how Coupa builds your approval chain from the management hierarchy, how comments flow back to the requester, and what happens when an approver delegates or doesn't act within 48 hours.

⏱
Going on vacation? Delegate.
Settings → Delegates lets you temporarily assign approval authority to a peer with equal or higher rights. You can choose to silence notifications during the delegation, or stay looped in. If approvers don't take action, Coupa nudges them every 48 hours until the approval is complete.

Module Five

Managing requests after submission

Things change. Coupa gives you the tools to withdraw, change, or approve invoices for the requests you've already submitted.

Pull a requisition back before it becomes a PO.

If you submitted a requisition that needs changes — and it hasn't yet been flipped into a Purchase Order — you can withdraw it, make corrections, and resubmit.

⚠️ Important: once a requisition has become a PO, withdraw is no longer available. Use a PO Change Request instead.

1
Go to Activity
From your username dropdown, click Activity. Optional tip: filter to "Pending Approval" or "Requiring Action."
2
Find the requisition
Locate the requisition you want to withdraw in the list.
3
Click the withdraw icon
The red arrow icon withdraws the requisition immediately and returns it to draft.
4
Edit and resubmit
Make your changes and submit the requisition again to restart the approval flow.

Change a PO that's already issued.

If a PO has been issued but not yet received, you can submit a PO Change Request. This is the only way to modify a PO after it's been sent to a supplier.

Note: change requests cannot be submitted on lines that have already been received.

1
Open the PO
Activity → Orders → click the PO number you need to change.
2
Click Request Change
Scroll to the bottom of the PO and click Request Change to unlock editable fields.
3
Edit and attach
Update quantities, prices, or other fields. Attach supplier docs (e.g., revised quotes) in the Attachments field.
4
Submit for approval
Click Submit Change Request. The change goes through approval before being sent to the supplier.

Approve invoices for what you ordered.

When a supplier submits an invoice against your PO, you'll be notified to review and approve it. Only the original requester is required to approve — not the full chain.

All service-based (amount) and goods-based (quantity) POs require invoice approval before payment is released.

  • Approve or reject after reviewing the invoice details.
  • Rejected invoices require a comment and are sent back to AP.
  • You can also find pending invoices under Activity → "Requiring My Approval."
1
Get notified
You'll receive a To Do notification when an invoice tied to your PO is ready for review.
2
Open the invoice
Click the notification to see line-level details, quantities, and amounts.
3
Verify against the PO
Confirm goods or services were received as expected. Catch any pricing or quantity discrepancies.
4
Approve or reject
If approved, payment moves forward. If rejected, add a comment — it returns to AP.

Module Six

A status reference for every state

Every requisition, PO, and invoice has a status that tells you exactly where it stands. Toggle between the three categories below to explore each one.

Draft
Items added to cart and saved for later. Standard requisition not yet submitted.
Cart
The active requisition — items currently in cart, waiting to be saved or submitted.
Pending Buyer Action
Submitted but blocked — required information is missing. The requester must correct before approvals route.
Pending Approval
Sent for approval and currently awaiting action from one or more users in the approval chain.
Approved
All approvals complete. Usually a fleeting status — the requisition moves quickly to Backgrounded or Ordered.
Backgrounded
Waiting in queue to be converted into a PO. Happens with large requests or busy system periods.
Ordered
Converted into a Purchase Order. From here you'll track activity under the PO instead.
Buyer Hold
PO approved but pending buyer review before transmission.
Cancelled
PO is cancelled and does not need to be fulfilled.
Closed
PO was received and then closed — manually or automatically by Coupa.
Currency Hold
On hold due to a currency exchange rate issue. Requires review before issuing.
Error
Something is wrong with the PO. Reach out to Procurement or Coupa Support to resolve.
Issued
PO was approved and successfully sent to the supplier.
Soft Closed
PO closed but reopenable. The supplier cannot invoice against a PO in this state.
Draft
Invoice entered in Coupa and saved, but not yet submitted for approval.
AP Hold
Supplier is flagged to "Hold for AP Review" — all invoices from them route here for AP first.
On Hold
Failed one or more Coupa invoice tolerances. AP determines the next step.
Pending Approval
Currently sitting with a business approver in the invoice approval process.
Pending Receipt
For 3-way match POs — receipt must be confirmed by the requester or Central Receiving.
Disputed
Sent back to the supplier for changes. Requires a dispute reason and notifies the supplier.
Rejected
Rejected by AP or a business approver. Sits with accounting supervisor for next steps.
Voided
AP Supervisor voided the invoice. It cannot be resubmitted.
Approved
Ready to be paid. Passed all tolerances and approvals. Picked up at the next AP integration run.

Module Seven

Mobile, security, and where to get help

Approve on the go, set up multi-factor authentication, and know who to contact when you're stuck.

ON THE GO

The Coupa Mobile App

Download from the App Store or Google Play. If you can access Coupa on the web, you can sign in on mobile. SSO works the same — enter your Coupa instance URL and you'll be redirected to your company login.

SECURITY

Multi-Factor Authentication

Required if you'll request payments or virtual cards. In Settings, click Enable, then scan the QR code with Duo Mobile (or another authenticator). Enter the code to finish setup. Done in 60 seconds.

SUPPORT

When you need help

Start with this training. Then attend Hypercare Office Hours (daily sessions during go-live), use the Teams support channel, or submit a help desk ticket. The Coupa Compass site has deeper documentation.
